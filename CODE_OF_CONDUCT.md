# Community Code of Conduct

Echo Canvas Web aims to make acoustic research, browser audio, and open-source contribution accessible to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. All participants deserve respectful treatment regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, nationality, appearance, religion, sexual orientation, or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient with questions and explain technical feedback clearly.
- Critique ideas and evidence, and remain respectful toward the people presenting them.
- Credit other contributors and preserve source and license attribution.
- Respect privacy, consent, and accessibility needs.
- Accept corrections and help resolve misunderstandings.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information without permission are not acceptable. The same rules apply to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, project events, and public spaces when someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a private contact method listed on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/kekekewww). If no suitable private channel is available, request a private contact method without posting details about the incident. Security vulnerabilities use the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review available evidence, protect reports from unnecessary disclosure, and respond proportionately. Responses may include a private warning, removal of harmful content, restrictions on participation, or a temporary or permanent ban. A maintainer involved in a report should not decide its outcome. Where feasible, the reporter and affected participant may request review by another uninvolved maintainer.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), but its text is specific to this project. It does not promise a response deadline or a confidentiality guarantee beyond the maintainers' ability to provide one.
